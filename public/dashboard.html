Bot Features

🎤 Voice Note Transcription LIVE

Customers send voice notes in Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Pidgin or English. Your bot automatically transcribes and replies in English text. Powered by OpenAI Whisper.

💰 Smart Order & Payment Flow LIVE

When a customer says they have paid, your bot asks for a receipt photo. The receipt is saved and you get alerted on your personal WhatsApp. Reply 1 to confirm, 2 to ask them wait, 3 if not received. After confirming, the bot collects the customer's delivery address automatically.

You can also manage orders directly from the Orders tab on this dashboard.

🤝 Customer Name Memory LIVE

The bot collects every customer's name on first contact and remembers it forever. Returning customers are greeted by name. All customer contacts are used for broadcasts.

🙌 Human Handoff Detection LIVE

When a customer asks to speak to a real person, the bot tells them to hold on, pauses itself for 30 minutes, and alerts you immediately on your personal WhatsApp.

📌 Lead Alerts LIVE

Every time a new customer asks for a price, your bot sends you a lead alert on WhatsApp with the customer's name, what they asked about, and a direct link to this dashboard. You always know who is interested.

🚀 Upcoming Upgrades — You joined ForgeBot early. All features below will be unlocked for you completely free when they go live. No extra payment needed.

Fake Payment Receipt Detection
