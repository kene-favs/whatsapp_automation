Bot Features

🏫 Context-Aware Media Sending LIVE

When a customer asks for photos or videos, the bot knows exactly which listing they are talking about and sends ONLY the media for that specific listing — one by one. No random files.

📄 PDF / Document Sending LIVE

Upload PDF brochures, catalogs, land documents, service menus, or any file to a listing. The bot sends them directly in WhatsApp when a customer requests them.

📍 Location Sharing LIVE

Set your office address and Google Maps link in Settings. When a customer asks "where are you located?" the bot sends your address and map link automatically.

🅾 Lead Qualification LIVE

When a customer shows buying intent, the bot asks targeted qualifying questions based on your business type (real estate, photography, consulting, etc.) and sends a full summary to you. Enable this in Settings.

🎤 Voice Note Transcription LIVE

Customers can send voice notes in Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Pidgin, or English. The bot transcribes them and replies in text automatically.

💰 Payment Alerts + Human Handoff LIVE

When a customer says they have paid, you get a WhatsApp alert. When they ask for a real person, the bot connects them to you immediately and pauses itself for 30 minutes.

🔒 Fake Receipt Detection COMING SOON

AI scans every payment screenshot customers send and flags manipulated receipts before you release goods or services.

📷 Social Media Bot + AI Video Creator COMING SOON

Same bot expands to Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook DMs. Plus AI-generated promo videos posted to your pages on a schedule.
