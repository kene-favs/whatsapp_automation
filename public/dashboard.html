Bot Features

🎙️ Voice Note Transcription LIVE

Customers send voice notes in Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Pidgin or English. Your bot automatically transcribes and replies in English text. Powered by OpenAI Whisper.

💰 Payment Confirmation Alerts LIVE

When a customer says they have paid, your bot alerts you on your personal WhatsApp. Reply 1 to confirm, 2 to ask them wait, 3 if not received. Bot messages the customer automatically.

Set your notification number in the Settings tab.

🙏 Human Handoff Detection LIVE

When a customer asks to speak to a real person, the bot tells them to hold on, pauses itself for 30 minutes, and alerts you immediately on your personal WhatsApp.

🔒

Fake Payment Receipt Detection

Our AI will automatically scan payment screenshots sent by customers and verify if they are real or edited. No more fake alerts fooling your business.
