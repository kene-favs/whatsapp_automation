Bot Capabilities

What the Bot Actually Does

When a customer sends a message, the bot reads it, understands what they want, and replies like a human. It even understands Nigerian Pidgin. Here's what it handles:

🤖 Auto-Replies (24/7)

  • Customer asks price → Bot replies with prices
  • Customer asks about availability → Bot checks and responds
  • "How far?" / "Abeg how much?" → Bot understands Pidgin
  • Delivery questions → Bot gives delivery info
  • Payment questions → Bot shares payment details

📸 Daily Status Posts

  • Posts product images to WhatsApp Status every day
  • Writes its own captions — no input needed from owner
  • Different caption every day — never repeats
  • Posts at whatever time is set (e.g. 9am daily)
  • Salon, food, fashion, cosmetics — all supported

📢 Customer Broadcasts

  • Send a message to ALL their customers at once
  • Announce sales, new products, promotions
  • Done from their dashboard — one click

🎛️ Client Dashboard

  • Client logs in to update their own products/prices
  • Add or change auto-reply messages anytime
  • Schedule status posts, view broadcast history
